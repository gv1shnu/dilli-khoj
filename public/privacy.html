Dilli Khoj

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 September 2026 · Last updated: 4 September 2026

Dilli Khoj (“the game”, “we”, “us”) is an educational browser game that teaches PostgreSQL by exploring the ruins of a fictional Delhi. It is open to anyone who signs in with a Google account. This policy explains what personal data the game processes, why, and the choices you have. It covers the game hosted at https://dilli-khoj.example.workers.dev.

1. Who we are

Dilli Khoj is operated by its maintainers as a non-commercial teaching tool. Anyone who signs in with a Google account can play.

2. What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive and store a limited profile:

  • Your email address — to identify your account and attribute your progress.
  • Your name and profile picture — as provided by Google, to show who you are in the game and on the leaderboard.
  • Your Google account identifier — a stable id used to link your sign-ins to one player record.

As you play, the game also stores gameplay data that you generate:

  • Which ruins you have surveyed and solved, and when.
  • Your experience-point (XP) balance, hints opened, and solutions revealed.
  • The SQL queries you submit for grading, and the grading outcome.
  • Your completion time, used only to order the leaderboard among players who finish.

We request only the Google scopes openid, email, and profile. We do not access your Google contacts, Drive, calendar, or any other Google data.

3. What we do not collect

  • We never ask for or store your Google password — authentication happens on Google’s own sign-in screen.
  • We do not collect payment information; the game is free to play.

4. Why we process this data (legal basis)

We process the data above to provide the game: to authenticate you, save your progress, grade your submissions, and rank completers on the leaderboard. Where required, the basis for processing is our legitimate interest in operating an educational game. You may withdraw by asking us to delete your account (see section 9).

5. How and where it is stored

Player accounts and progress are stored in a managed Supabase Postgres database, and the site is served through Cloudflare. These providers process the data on our behalf as sub-processors. Access to raw player records is restricted to course maintainers (“admins”), and every administrative read of player data is written to an audit log.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising. Your name and rank become visible to other players only after you complete all ruins and appear on the leaderboard. Otherwise, your data is shared only with the infrastructure providers named above and with course maintainers for running the game and supporting the course.

7. Retention

We keep your account and gameplay data for as long as the course runs and for a reasonable period afterwards for academic record-keeping, after which it may be deleted or anonymised. You can request earlier deletion at any time.

8. Security

Sign-in uses Google OAuth with PKCE; we never see your Google credentials. Database access is governed by row-level security so players can read and write only their own records, and administrative access is limited and audited. No online service can promise perfect security, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

9. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to export your gameplay record. To exercise any of these, contact us using the details below. Because sign-in is tied to your Google account, we may verify your identity through that account before acting.

10. Children

The game is intended for a general audience of SQL learners. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game evolves. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, announced through the course.
