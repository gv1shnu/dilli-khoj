# Full game walkthrough notes

Date: 2026-09-05  
Branch: `open-world`  
Starting revision: `6c7a0b8`

This is the live review log for a slow, sequential playthrough from Ruin 01 to Ruin 20. Each ruin is checked through the player-facing game: arrival, physical route, amber trail, archive interaction, visible result, restored gate, exit route, transition, and ambience profile change.

User comments made during the walkthrough are recorded under the ruin where they were raised. Passing solutions are intentionally omitted from this document.

## Ruin 01

- Status: completed; transitioned sequentially to Ruin 02
- Arrival/environment: Purana Qila quarantine courtyard; title and area number were consistent across the HUD.
- Amber/archive: the trail led to Archive 01 and stopped within interaction range; the archive opened from the in-world prompt.
- Archive check: the visible task passed and returned the expected three-row result.
- Restoration/exit: progress changed to 1/20, the archive control disabled, the mission changed, and the ground trail reversed toward the opened gate.
- Transition/ambience: the gate advanced to Ruin 02 and the configured profile changed from “Fortress wind · distant bells” to “Depot draft · loose metal”.
- Observation: the translucent exit surface filled a large part of the camera view while the character passed through it; review whether the portal should be subtler or thinner.

## Ruin 02

- Status: completed; transitioned sequentially to Ruin 03
- Arrival/environment: Kashmere Gate bus depot, with buses, platforms, departure signage, lamps, and road markings clearly distinct from Ruin 01.
- Amber/archive: the pathfinder followed the trail through the depot and reached Archive 02 without collision or manual steering.
- Archive check: the visible task passed with the expected two-row result.
- Restoration/exit: progress changed to 2/20 and the trail redirected across the depot to the open gate.
- Transition/ambience: the gate advanced to Ruin 03 and the configured profile changed from “Depot draft · loose metal” to “River wash · water birds”.

## Ruin 03

- Status: completed; transitioned sequentially to Ruin 04
- Arrival/environment: Nigambodh Ghat presented an open riverside court, stepped masonry, arches, and water, clearly distinct from the depot.
- Amber/archive: the trail stayed associated with Archive 03 and the walking route reached it without collision.
- Archive check: the visible task passed with the expected two-row result.
- Restoration/exit: progress changed to 3/20 and the route led through the opened gate.
- Transition/ambience: the configured profile changed from “River wash · water birds” to “Office draft · creaking wood” on entry to Ruin 04.

## Ruin 04

- Status: completed; transitioned sequentially to Ruin 05
- Arrival/environment: Old Delhi records room used a walled office-yard layout with desks, scattered debris, lamps, and trees.
- Amber/archive: the guided walking route reached Archive 04 without collision.
- Archive check: the visible task passed with the expected two-row result.
- Restoration/exit: progress changed to 4/20 and the exit trail led to a working gate.
- Transition/ambience: the configured profile changed from “Office draft · creaking wood” to “Vault resonance · distant drips” on entry to Ruin 05.
- Observation: as in Ruin 01, the translucent exit surface dominated the camera while crossing the gate.

## Ruin 05

- Status: completed; transitioned sequentially to Ruin 06
- Arrival/environment: Kotwali vault used a sparse fortified enclosure with heavy walls, strong shadows, and paired lamps.
- Amber/archive: the trail led directly to Archive 05 and enabled only its matching interaction.
- Archive check: the visible task passed with the expected three-row result.
- Restoration/exit: progress changed to 5/20 and the restored route crossed the gate successfully.
- Transition/ambience: the configured profile changed from “Vault resonance · distant drips” to “Bazaar breeze · timber shutters” on entry to Ruin 06.

## Ruin 06

- Status: walkthrough stopped after its archive was restored; exit crossing was not reviewed
- Arrival/environment: Chandni Chowk used narrow market lanes, stalls, striped awnings, arches, and overhead signs.
- Amber/archive: the walking route reached Archive 06 and its visible task passed with the expected two-row result.
- User comments: the environmental ambience sounded the same across ruins; XP changes were not visibly animating above the avatar; normal movement should run and Shift should walk.
- Follow-up: walkthrough paused at the user's request while these items are developed.
- Resolution: twenty place-specific ambience cues now play on entry and recur; solve/help XP changes are emitted directly by those actions and projected above the avatar; manual movement now runs by default and Shift selects walking.
