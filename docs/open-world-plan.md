# Open-world implementation

Approved scope: 20 individually authored physical locations across seven districts,
sequential physical area and archive unlocking, new environmental ambience,
route guidance, visible restoration, restored-location travel, and plain-language
question descriptions without SQL keywords. Work remains on `open-world`.

## Checkpoints

- Shared world layout, reusable original asset kit, movement and archive integration.
- One commit for each completed location, in curriculum order (01–20).
- Whole-city integration, gameplay regression checks, production asset safety.

Each level needs an identifiable silhouette, a distinct route/footprint, a permanent
archive, optional discovery, its own ambient profile, and a traversable entrance.
Major architecture is authored per level; small props/materials are reused.
All new geometry, signage and synthesized sounds are generated by project code;
no new third-party assets or paid services are required.

## Invariants

Server progression is authoritative. Practice Run cannot unlock live areas.
Locked map entries, physical areas and archives stay inaccessible.
Canonical solutions and private hints remain outside production assets.
The Soldier faces -Z; retain its PI orientation correction and guide correction.
Retain seamless toroidal wrapping; do not add a world-radius clamp.
No production deployment or live database migrations in this work.
